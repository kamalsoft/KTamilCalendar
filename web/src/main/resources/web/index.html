Panchangam values are computed from Sun and Moon positions and checked against the Swiss Ephemeris. Printed calendars in your area may differ by a few minutes or a day. Festival and holiday dates follow official lists where available; Islamic dates depend on local moon sighting.

Accuracy. The astronomy is checked against the Swiss Ephemeris, but the traditional rules (Muhurtham, Lagna, Thara, Chandrashtamam, Sraardham) are calculated, not issued by a religious authority. Before a marriage, ceremony, Sraardham or any muhurtham, confirm the date and time with a qualified temple priest.
