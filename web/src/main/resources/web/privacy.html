Privacy Policy

Effective 15 November 2026. Panchangam is developed by kamalsoft.

Summary

Panchangam has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your settings and places stay on your device. The app contacts only the services listed below, and only to get the data it needs for the screen you are using.

What is stored on your device

  • Settings: theme, which tabs and faiths are shown, reminder choices, and the place you chose.
  • Birth details you enter (birth star and Rasi), used only for personal rules such as Thara Balam and Chandrashtamam.
  • The calendar, and the weather last fetched, kept so the app works offline.

On Android TV this is kept in the app's private storage. In the web app it is kept in your browser (localStorage and the browser cache). Nothing here is sent to the developer.

Location

  • Android TV: if you allow location, the app uses the TV's coarse location to pick a place. It is stored on the TV only. If you do not allow it, the app asks ipapi.co for an approximate city. That service receives the IP address of your connection.
  • Web app: only when you choose to use your location, the browser gives the app your coordinates. They are sent to api-bdc.net to get the name of the nearest town. Nothing is sent otherwise.

Weather and place search

To show the weather and to search for places, the app sends the place's coordinates or the search text to Open-Meteo (api.open-meteo.com and geocoding-api.open-meteo.com). Open-Meteo's own privacy policy applies to those requests.

Fonts and links

The web app loads the Tamil font from Google Fonts. Google receives your IP address for that request. The About page links to Thiruppugazh, an external site. Its privacy policy applies once you open it.

Notifications and reminders

Reminders (Rahu Kalam and good-time windows) are scheduled on your device. They do not send data anywhere. You can turn them off in Settings.

Android backup

The Android app allows the system backup. If your device backs up app data to your Google account, the settings and places may be included in that backup. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes the local copy.

Your choices

  • Deny location, or choose a place from the list.
  • Turn reminders off, or reset the settings (Settings → Reset all settings in the web app).
  • Clear the browser's site data for the web app, or uninstall the Android app, to delete everything it stored.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too, and the new version is published on this page.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue at github.com/kamalsoft/KTamilCalendar/issues.
